Dr. Nitin Mohan is a Consultant Paediatric Gastroenterologist, Hepatologist and Nutritionist at Churamani Multispeciality Hospital, Hisar. His qualifications include MBBS, MD (Paediatrics), FISPGHAN and MNNF.

His academic and professional journey is associated with prestigious medical institutions, including PGIMS Rohtak, Sir Ganga Ram Hospital, New Delhi, and ABVIMS & Dr. RML Hospital, New Delhi. His clinical focus includes paediatric digestive disorders, liver diseases, pancreatic conditions, feeding difficulties, poor weight gain and nutritional problems.
